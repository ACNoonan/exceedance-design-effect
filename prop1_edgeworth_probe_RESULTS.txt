VERDICT: INERT. The plain continuity-corrected normal reaches Proposition 1's
coefficient, and its own error lands inside the claimed O(n^-2) remainder. The
skewness term therefore only has to NOT CONTRIBUTE at O(1/n) -- which is what
the sketch's int phi(z)(z^2-1) dz = 0 says -- rather than to be expanded with a
bounded remainder. The remaining obligation is a SUMMED Berry-Esseen bound,
sum_l [P(A_l <= k-1) - Phi(z_l)] = O(M^-1), for hypergeometric CDFs along a
linear boundary. Pointwise is classical (Hoeglund 1978); summed is the work.
