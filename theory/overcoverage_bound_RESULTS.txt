VERDICT
    The over-coverage bound DOES fail. Crossover sits between m = 12 and m = 24;
    the rows either side of it turn on the ceiling remainder in k rather than on the
    drift, so the honest statement is a crossover region, not a single m. It fails
    identically for the tie-free model, and a copula matched on rho_I with the
    opposite drift sign stays inside the bound at the same m — so what breaks it is
    the sign of rho_I', with m only shrinking the slack it has to beat.
