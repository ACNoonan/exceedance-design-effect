VERDICT
  Marginal coverage is not k/(n+1) under (A1)+(A3). In the interior -- [1] and [2],
  one-factor Gaussian and atom mixture alike -- the >= 1-alpha guarantee itself fails in
  every configuration tested, by 0.11 points at SS6.1's own b=100 x m=10, rho=0.5.
  At the perfectly-tied endpoint [4] the ceiling in ceil(k/m) usually pushes coverage
  the other way and the guarantee holds (3 of the 4 shown); the fourth fails by 0.69
  points, which is the rigorous, expansion-free half of the finding.
  The failure is O(1/b) and second-order; the dispersion failure is first-order and
  remains what the paper is for. The configuration in which the guarantee DOES survive
  is the one where the test point shares the dependence (arm D) -- SS3.3's benign case,
  not SS6.1's.
