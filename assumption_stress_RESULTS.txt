    -> the rival is NOT conservative by design: counterexample found
    At q=0.5 the score correlation is exactly 0 — a practitioner applying the rival
    applies no correction at all — while rho_I(p) > 0 and the true design effect
    exceeds 1. Throughout 0.10 < q < 0.50 the score correlation is NEGATIVE,
    which reads as a variance bonus, while the true design effect still exceeds 1.
    Below q = 1 - p = 0.10 the two agree in sign and the bonus is real:
    rho_I(p) = [q - (1-p)]/p, so the crossing is exactly at q = 1 - p.
